About Us

Our Company

Ceylon Blue Hues operates as a US-HEADQUARTERED organization with significant historical ties to SRI LANKA, having established itself as a prominent player in the GEMSTONE TRADE.

Our foundation was built on relationships with SMALL-SCALE MINERS operating in rural areas of Sri Lanka. We subsequently diversified our sourcing to include ARTISANAL SMALL MINERS from numerous locations across the island nation.

We now partner with over 400 SMALL-SCALE MINING ENTERPRISES throughout Sri Lanka. The PRIMARY STAKEHOLDERS benefiting from Ceylon Blue Hues are the small-scale rural mining communities. Each colored stone purchased from us ensures that these miners receive a proportionate and FAIR PAYMENT for their hard work and MINING EXPERTISE.

Our Heritage

The island has a long and distinguished history as a source of some of the world's most remarkable sapphires, with a tradition that has shaped local communities, inspired generations and contributed to Sri Lanka’s cultural identity.

This enduring heritage reflects not only the natural richness of the land but also the skill, dedication and knowledge of the people who have worked with these extraordinary gems for centuries.

An Exceptional Find

Sapphires sourced from Sri Lanka. The radiant beauty of nature’s most precious gems.

Sri Lanka-sourced rough sapphires represent stones from one of the planet’s most distinguished regions for sapphire color variety and abundance.

Prior to the cutting and imaginative craftsmanship that will eventually animate these gems within jewelry designs, every stone already exhibits its own singular character and natural allure.

Our objective is to position Ceylon blue sapphires—acknowledged worldwide for their exceptional rarity and distinctive color—in the marketplace at equitable pricing that remains competitive with other North American vendors.

Guided by our fundamental values and commitment to ensuring that colored gemstones deliver substantial benefits to the communities and mining populations from which they are sourced.

Preserving the Heritage of Gemstones

Our commitment to preserving Sri Lanka’s gemstone heritage is rooted in respect for the land, the people and the time-honored knowledge that has sustained this tradition for generations.

By supporting responsible practices and celebrating artisanal expertise, we help ensure that Ceylon’s extraordinary sapphires continue to be valued for generations to come.

The Genesis of Ceylon Sapphire Gemstones

The exact chronology of sapphire gemstone formation remains uncertain. Nevertheless, sapphires found in contemporary geological contexts are believed to have formed between 450 and 750 million years ago.
